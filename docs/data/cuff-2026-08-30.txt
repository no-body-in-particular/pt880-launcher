between 66 and 71. A cuff diastolic is the least repeatable number either device
produces, so it is left out of the mean and left in the record.

So the intercepts move by five and six, and nothing else moves. Seven points
against twelve places an offset; it does not fit a slope. An offset found on one
wearer on one afternoon is exactly that, and the coefficients it sits on top of
are still the unfitted ones taken from the literature.
